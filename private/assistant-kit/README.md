# Assistant Team Starter Kit by Prompt Forge Kits

**Ready-made jobs, routines and safety rules for a small team of AI assistants that help you run a small online product business.**

Written for Grok Bot. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the makers of Grok Bot.

Version 1.0 · © 2026 Prompt Forge Kits · promptforgekits.com

## Who this is for
You sell a digital product online: a template, a kit, an ebook, a course, a small app. You are not a coder. You want AI assistants to handle the daily busywork (check the shop, draft posts, watch for feedback, write the weekly report). **You stay the one who approves anything public, anything sent to a person, and anything that costs money.**

## The team at a glance
| Bot | Main job | Scheduled jobs |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | Keeps the plan, writes the weekly report, turns open questions into small yes/no decisions for you | Weekly report (Monday) |
| **Content** | Drafts posts, the newsletter, blog posts and store copy. Never publishes | Weekly newsletter draft (Tuesday) |
| **Community & Launch** | Watches for questions, feedback and bugs. Posts only what you approved | Feedback watch (daily), post approved posts (twice a day) |
| **Shop Ops** | Checks the site, checkout, downloads and listings every day. Prepares fixes as drafts | Daily ops check, fact sync check (Thursday) |

You can start with one Bot (Shop Ops) and add the others later. `START-HERE.md` shows how.

## How it stays safe
- Every Bot gets the same approval rules. Posting, sending, publishing, deploying, spending, deleting and replying to customers all wait for your OK.
- Public posts go through one queue. Content drafts them, you approve, and Community & Launch posts **only** approved items, exactly as written, after checking that the post isn't already up (the "duplicate guard").
- Every job writes a short entry in a shared notebook, so one page a day tells you what happened.
- Every job is done once by hand, and checked by you, before it gets a schedule.

## What you need
- A Grok Bot account (a separate subscription, not included in this kit).
- The apps you already use for your business, connected in Grok Bot where you want help (for example your email tool or your X account). The kit works with fewer apps; each job says what it uses and what to do without it.
- One to two hours for setup, then about ten minutes a day to read and approve.

## What this kit is not
- Not a promise of sales or income. The assistants do the work you describe; results depend on your product and your audience.
- Not autopilot. It is built so that you approve the important steps.
- Not legal, tax or financial advice.
- Not an official Grok Bot product. Grok Bot features and screens can change; the kit tells you what to *ask* your Bots, which keeps working when buttons move.
