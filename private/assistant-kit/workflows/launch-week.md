# Launch week  (owner: Lead, with Content, Community & Launch and Shop Ops)

**Uses:** the shared notes, your website, and the apps the other jobs already use. Run this only when you launch something new (a product, a big update, a sale you have approved).

## Part A. The job (send it when you plan a launch; save it as a skill called "Launch week" after the first time)

We are launching: {{WHAT}} on {{DATE}} ({{TIME_ZONE}}). Read `BUSINESS-PROFILE.md` first.

1. **Facts first.** List the launch facts (name, what's inside, price, refund, link). Ask the owner to confirm them. Add any new fact to `BUSINESS-PROFILE.md` only after the owner confirms it.
2. **Ready check (Shop Ops).** Ask Shop Ops to run the Daily ops check and the Fact sync check on the new page, the checkout (stopping before payment) and the delivery. Everything that blocks a buyer must be fixed and approved before launch day.
3. **Posts (Content).** Ask Content for 5-7 launch post drafts in `POST-QUEUE.md`: useful on their own (a tip, a behind-the-scenes look, a short demo), each one passed through the True-claims check, spaced over the week with `Post after` times. No fake deadlines, no fake discounts.
4. **Newsletter (Content).** One launch email draft in `shop-notes/newsletter/`, marked NOT SENT.
5. **Owner decisions.** Put one item in `DECISIONS-NEEDED.md` with everything the owner must approve: the page going live, each post, the email. Each item is answerable with yes / no.
6. **Launch day (Community & Launch).** Post only approved posts, with the duplicate guard. Run the Feedback watch twice that day and flag buyer questions as `URGENT`.
7. **Day 7 review (Lead).** One page: what went out (links), real numbers (or "no access"), buyer problems and how they were handled, and what to change.

## Part B. No routine
Launches are one-off. Instead, ask Lead: "On {{DATE}}, remind me to run the Launch week day 7 review." Keep the other routines running as usual.
