# Weekly newsletter draft  (owner: Content)

**Uses:** the shared notes. Optional: your email tool, to read the subscriber count and save an unsent draft.

## Part A. The job (send it, run it once by hand, then say "Save the process we just used as a skill called Weekly newsletter draft")

Draft this week's newsletter. Never send it.

1. Read `BUSINESS-PROFILE.md`, this week's `CHANGELOG.md` (what changed in the product) and `FEEDBACK.md` (what people asked).
2. If you can see the email tool, note how many subscribers are in the list. Don't export or copy their addresses.
3. Draft:
   - **Subject** (under 50 characters) and **preview line**.
   - **What's new** (only real changes this week, from `CHANGELOG.md`).
   - **One useful tip** a reader can use today, even without buying anything.
   - **One featured thing** (a free resource, a demo, a customer project shared with permission).
   - **One soft mention** of the paid product, with a link.
   - **Reply invitation** ("Stuck on something? Reply and tell us").
   - **Unsubscribe line** (use your email tool's unsubscribe placeholder).
4. Run the **True-claims check**.
5. Save as `shop-notes/newsletter/YYYY-MM-DD.md` with `NOT SENT, needs owner approval` at the top. If the email tool is connected, you may also save it there as an unsent draft.
6. Add one item to `DECISIONS-NEEDED.md`: "Approve newsletter for YYYY-MM-DD?" with the subject line.

If nothing real changed this week, say so and suggest skipping the week. A skipped week is better than filler.

## Part B. The routine (send to the owning Bot, then test it once)

> Every Tuesday at 10:00 AM {{TIME_ZONE}}, run the Weekly newsletter draft skill. Never send, schedule or test-send to the list.
