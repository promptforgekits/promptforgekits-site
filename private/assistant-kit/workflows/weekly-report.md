# Weekly report  (owner: Lead)

**Uses:** the shared notes. Optional: read-only access to your store or payment dashboard and your email tool's subscriber count. Without them, numbers say "no access".

## Part A. The job (send it, run it once by hand, then say "Save the process we just used as a skill called Weekly report")

Write the owner a one-page report of last week (Monday to Sunday). Read `BUSINESS-PROFILE.md`, then last week's entries in `CHANGELOG.md`, `FEEDBACK.md`, `POSTED-LOG.md`, `IDEAS.md` and `DECISIONS-NEEDED.md`.

Sections (keep each to a few lines):
1. **Customers first:** any buyer problems, how they were handled, anything still open.
2. **Numbers:** sales, visitors, new subscribers, followers, if you have access. Write "no access" or "unknown" rather than guessing. Compare to last week only when both numbers are real.
3. **What the team did:** one line per assistant.
4. **What went out publicly:** posts (with links), newsletter, site changes.
5. **What broke and what was fixed.**
6. **Decisions needed:** at most 3, each answerable with yes / no / pick one, with your recommendation.
7. **Next week:** the top 3 things the team will do.

Rules: facts with a source (file or link); guesses labeled as guesses. No hype.

Output: save as `shop-notes/reports/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly.md` and post the "Decisions needed" section here.

## Part B. The routine (send to the owning Bot, then test it once)

> Every Monday at 8:00 AM {{TIME_ZONE}}, run the Weekly report skill for last week. Post the decisions-needed section here. Don't contact anyone.
